I am an Economist a Strategist a Public Voice

Matthew T. Patton, Ph.D.

Strategy executive and former academic with 15+ years turning rigorous economic, institutional, and workforce research into decisions that improve outcomes, for Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, universities, and communities in transition. As Executive Vice President at AngelouEconomics, I've directed a $9M+ project portfolio spanning site selection, economic diversification, and institutional strategy across 30+ states and countries. Earlier in my career, I led curriculum design, grant-funded STEM initiatives, and academic advising in higher education.

What ties both halves of my career together: I build the analytical frameworks, IMPLAN modeling, GIS, quantitative and qualitative research, to prove what's actually working, then translate that evidence into strategy that boards, legislators, and communities can act on. I've helped a coal-dependent town reinvent its economy, advised university systems through institutional change, and testified before the Texas Legislature. I bring the same discipline, evidence first, people-centered outcomes always, whether the audience is a corporate boardroom or a classroom.
